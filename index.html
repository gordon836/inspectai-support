How the app works

  1. Tap "New inspection" and enter the property address.
  2. Select the inspection photos from your photo library. You can select 100 or more at once.
  3. The app resizes the photos and sorts them into areas of the property. Check the areas and move any photo that is in the wrong one.
  4. The app analyses each area for defects.
  5. Review each finding: confirm it, edit it, or mark it as not a defect.
  6. Preview the report, then share or email the PDF.

Common questions

How long does an inspection take to process?
About 1 minute for every 30 photos, depending on your connection.

Do I have to keep the app open?
The screen stays on while the app is working. If you switch to another app, the work pauses and carries on when you come back. If the app is closed, open it again and choose the draft on the home screen.

A photo was sorted into the wrong area.
On the areas screen, select the photo and move it to the right area before starting the analysis.

A finding is wrong.
Mark it "Not a defect" and it is left out of the report. You can also edit the wording, severity and cost of any finding. The app prepares a draft. The inspector decides what goes in the report.

How selecting many photos works.
In the photo picker, touch the first photo, slide sideways, then drag down to select whole rows.

The report will not send by email.
Reports are kept under 20 MB. If your mail app still refuses the attachment, use "Share" and send it another way, such as a cloud link.

How do I delete my account or an inspection?
Email us from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.

Important

InspectAI works from photographs only. It is a drafting tool for building inspectors and does not replace the inspector's own inspection, judgement or sign-off.
