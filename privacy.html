InspectAI Building Inspection: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

InspectAI Building Inspection ("the app") is provided by Akai Renga Pty Ltd ("we", "us"). The app is a tool for building inspectors. It turns the photos from a building inspection into a draft defect report. This policy sets out what information the app collects, why, and who it is shared with. We comply with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

1. What the app collects

  • Account details. Your name and email address, used to sign in. Your password is held by our sign-in provider in hashed form. We cannot read it.
  • Inspection photos. The photos you select from your photo library for an inspection. The app only sees the photos you choose.
  • Inspection details you enter. The property address, property and inspection type, and the client's name and email if you add them.
  • Location. Your device's location, only at the moment you tap "Use my current location" to fill in the property address. The app does not record location at any other time.
  • Findings and reports. The defects the AI identifies, your edits and decisions on them, and the reports you produce.

The app does not use advertising, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not collect analytics about how you use it.

2. How the information is used

  • To sign you in and keep your inspections separate from other users'.
  • To sort inspection photos into areas of the property and identify building defects.
  • To build, display, share and email inspection reports at your direction.
  • To let you reopen an earlier inspection and its report.

We do not use your information for marketing and we do not sell it.

3. Who the information is shared with

The app relies on 2 service providers to work:

  • Anthropic, PBC (United States). Inspection photos and the property type are sent to Anthropic's Claude service for analysis. Your name, email and the client's details are not sent. Anthropic states that it does not use data sent through its commercial API to train its models. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase (data stored in Sydney, Australia). Your account, inspection details, findings and photos are stored in our Supabase project. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.

Because Anthropic processes photos in the United States, inspection photos are disclosed overseas for that purpose. We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

4. Photos of other people's property

Inspection photos usually show a property owned or occupied by someone other than you. You are responsible for having the authority to photograph the property and to prepare a report on it. Avoid capturing people, documents or personal items in inspection photos where you can.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

Photos and drafts are held on your device while you work, and inspections you complete are stored in our Supabase project so you can reopen them. We keep inspection records until you ask us to delete them, or for as long as the law or professional record-keeping obligations require.

You can delete your account yourself at any time. In the app, open the Profile tab and tap "Delete account". This permanently removes your login, your inspections, the findings and the photos stored for them, online and on your phone. Reports you have already emailed or shared are not affected. If you cannot use the app, email us from the address on your account at gordon.xue@jimsbuildinginspections.com.au and we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each user can only read their own inspections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that a breach will never occur. If one affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

7. Children

The app is a professional tool. It is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Access, correction and complaints

You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing the address below. If you have a complaint about how we have handled your information, contact us first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at www.oaic.gov.au.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
